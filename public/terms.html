Do not use the app in an emergency. If you have chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, confusion, stroke symptoms, very high or very low blood sugar with symptoms, or any other emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital straight away.

Medical advice

Medical advice, diagnosis and prescriptions come only from Dr. Ashwani Kansal, as the treating doctor, after he has assessed you. The app is software that connects you to him. Reminders, the general information in the app and the clinic assistant chat are not a consultation and do not replace a visit. Never start, stop or change a medicine because of anything other than your doctor's advice.

Clinic assistant

The clinic assistant is an automated chat that uses an AI model run by Groq. It can show what your doctor prescribed, check your bookings and payments, and fill in a visit booking or your readings for you to review. It does not diagnose, give medical advice or change medicines, and its replies can be wrong. Nothing is booked, paid or sent to your doctor until you check it and confirm it in the app. Use Report this reply under any answer that looks wrong or harmful.

Payments and the consultation unlock

Online consultations and clinic visit bookings are paid for through Razorpay. The app never sees your card, UPI or bank details. After your online consultation payment is confirmed, WhatsApp consultation with the doctor opens. Confirmation is automatic, or the clinic confirms it by hand if you paid through the payment page. A clinic visit is booked once its payment goes through. If your payment is taken but the consultation does not open or the visit is not booked, contact us and see the Refund policy.
