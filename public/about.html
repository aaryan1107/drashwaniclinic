About the clinic app

The Dr. Ashwani Family Clinic app helps patients book appointments, share health readings, receive prescriptions and follow-up plans, and pay for online consultations. The clinic provides care for diabetes, obesity, thyroid, hypertension, OCD and anxiety.

Consultations are held at Dr. Ashwani Family Clinic, 1st Floor, Shop Number 1011, Ace City Square Market, Ace City Road, Jalalpur, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh 203207, India, and at Yatharth Super Speciality Hospital, Greater Noida. Timings are shown live in the app and can change.
