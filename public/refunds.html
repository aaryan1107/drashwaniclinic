Refund policy

Effective 22 November 2026 (version 1.0)

This policy covers the two fees the app collects: the online consultation fee (Rs 800, or Rs 600 for patients aged 60 and over) and the clinic visit booking fee (Rs 700).

When you get a refund

  • The clinic cancels the consultation or visit, or cannot hold it.
  • Your payment was taken but the consultation did not open or the visit was not booked, or the payment was taken more than once for the same consultation or visit.
  • The payment failed on our side after your bank charged you.

When you do not

The fee is not refundable once the consultation or visit has taken place.

How to ask

Email aaryankansal06@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment, from the email address on your account. Include the date and the payment reference (the Razorpay payment number shown in your payment confirmation). Please do not send card or bank details.

How long it takes

We reply within 48 hours. An approved refund is sent to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take additional time to show it.
