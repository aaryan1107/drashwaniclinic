Request account deletion

Effective 23 November 2026 (version 1.1)

You can ask us to delete your account and the app data linked to it at any time.

Email an account deletion request

How to ask

Email aaryankansal06@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or use Request account deletion in the app's profile menu, which brings you to this page. If you signed up with a phone number, say so and we will verify you another way. Please keep prescriptions, identity documents, passwords and payment details out of the email.

How we verify you

We reply to the email address on your account, and we may ask you to confirm with a code sent to your account email or phone. We never ask for your password or your card details.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in account and your profile: name, email, phone number and photo link.
  • The health readings and reports you submitted.
  • Your sign-in record.

Reminders on your phone are cleared when you sign out, and everything on the phone goes when you uninstall the app. Copies held in our provider's routine backups are removed when those backups expire.

What we keep and why

We keep these records in restricted form, which means they are no longer linked to a login and can be seen only by the treating doctor and approved front-desk staff:

  • Clinical records (medical profile, appointments, prescriptions, care plans and visit records), because a doctor must keep a record of treatment. We keep them for 3 years after your last visit, then delete them. This is the clinic's own period, and we may keep a record longer only if the law or the medical council requires it, in which case we will tell you.
  • Payment records, for as long as tax and payment laws require. Razorpay keeps its own records under its own policy.
  • A note of your request: the date and your email address, and that we completed it, so that we can show we handled it.

We use kept records for nothing except these duties.

How long it takes

We acknowledge your request within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days of verifying you. We confirm by email when it is done.
