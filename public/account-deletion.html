Delete your account

Effective 24 November 2026 (version 1.2)

You can delete your account and the app data linked to it yourself, at any time, from inside the app. You don't need to email us, and it happens straight away.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the app, or the web app at drashwaniclinic.pages.dev, and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete account.

You are signed out and your account is gone. Deletion cannot be undone; if you come back later you will need a new account.

If you can no longer sign in, email aaryankansal06@gmail.com from the email address on your account and we will delete it for you within 7 days. We may ask you to confirm with a code sent to that email. We never ask for your password or card details.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile: name, email, phone number and photo link.
  • Your medical profile and patient record.
  • Your health readings and reports, appointments and bookings.
  • Your prescriptions and their PDF copies, your care plan and your visit records.
  • Your sign-in history.

Reminders on your phone are cleared when the app signs you out, and everything on the phone goes when you uninstall the app. Copies in our provider's routine backups are removed when those backups expire.

What we keep and why

The Income-tax Rules require a doctor to keep a register of fees received for 7 years. So for each payment you made we keep only your name, the amount, the date, what it was for and the Razorpay payment reference. Your email, phone number and the link to your account are removed. We use this record for nothing except tax and accounts, and delete it when the 7 years end. Razorpay keeps its own payment records under its own policy.

Doctor and front-desk accounts are removed by the clinic, not from this page.
