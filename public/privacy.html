Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026 (version 1.2)

In short

  • We collect your name and contact details, your health information, your appointments and your consultation payments.
  • This is health information. It is stored so your doctor can look after you, and for nothing else.
  • Only your doctor, approved front-desk staff and the app administrator can see it, and each only for the reasons below.
  • We do not sell it or use it for advertising. If you use the clinic assistant chat, your messages and the records it looks up are sent to Groq, an AI service in the United States, only to write the reply, and are not used to train AI.
  • You can ask us to correct it or give you a copy, and you can delete your account yourself in the app at any time.

Why we use it

We use your information only for these purposes:

  • To run your account: to sign you in and keep your profile.
  • To give you care: so your doctor can review your health, write prescriptions and care plans, and plan follow-ups.
  • To book appointments: to confirm a clinic visit or an online consultation.
  • To take and reconcile fees: to collect the online consultation fee and the clinic visit booking fee, unlock the consultation or confirm the booking after payment, and deal with refunds.
  • To answer you in the clinic assistant: to show what your doctor prescribed, fill in a booking or your readings for you to check, and answer questions about the app. Using the assistant is optional.
  • To keep the service secure: to prevent misuse and investigate problems, using the sign-in record and visit logs.
  • To meet legal duties: including medical record-keeping, tax and payment rules.

We ask for your consent to hold your health information when you create your medical profile. You can say no, but then we cannot provide the service. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see Your rights).

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not rent it or trade it.
  • We do not use it for advertising or marketing, and we do not send marketing messages. The only messages are care reminders and follow-ups that you turn on.
  • We do not use it for analytics or profiling. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-tracking tools.
  • We do not use it to train AI. The only AI service we use is the clinic assistant described under Who receives it. It never diagnoses, gives medical advice or changes medicines, and it cannot book or pay for anything without you confirming in the app.
  • We do not share it with insurers, employers, banks or data brokers.
  • We do not use it for any purpose other than the ones listed under Why we use it. If that ever needs to change, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Who receives it

We use these service providers to run the app. They handle your information to provide their service to us, under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Supabase provides the database, sign-in and the server functions where your records are stored. Supabase may use its own sub-processors, which it lists on its website.
  • Google processes your sign-in only if you choose Continue with Google. Google learns that you signed in to this app.
  • Razorpay processes your consultation and clinic visit payments. We receive the payment reference, amount and status.
  • Groq runs the AI model that writes the clinic assistant's replies, only when you send a message in the assistant. It receives your message, the last few messages of that chat and the records the assistant looks up to answer you. Under its terms Groq does not use this content to train AI models, and we have turned on Groq's Zero Data Retention setting, so it does not keep the content after the reply is written. Groq processes the content in the United States, outside India.
  • Cloudflare hosts the web version of the app and these pages. Like any website host, it sees ordinary request information such as your IP address.
  • WhatsApp is used only when you tap a WhatsApp link, or when the doctor messages you on the number you gave. WhatsApp handles those messages under its own policy.
  • Google Maps opens only when you tap a directions link for a clinic.
  • ABHA (the government health ID service) opens only if you tap its link to look up your ABHA number.

We may also disclose information where the law or a court or authority requires it. We do not disclose it to anyone else.

Where it is stored

Your records are stored on servers in Seoul, South Korea, which is outside India. Our provider may also handle some information, such as logs or support requests, in other countries. We use these providers because they are needed to run the app, and we rely on your consent, which you give when you create your account, to store your data outside India.

Who can see your records

  • Dr. Ashwani Kansal, your treating doctor, can see your records.
  • Front-desk staff approved by the clinic can see prescriptions, to search and print them. They cannot issue, change or cancel them.
  • The app administrator, Aaryan Kansal, can reach stored data in order to run, fix and secure the service and to answer your privacy requests. The administrator does not use it for any other purpose.
  • Other patients cannot see your information.
  • The doctor's private visit notes are shown only to the doctor in the app. They are never shown to patients and never printed on prescriptions. The administrator's technical access described above also covers stored data of this kind.

Children

Patients under 18 can use the app only through an account created and managed by a parent or guardian, who gives consent for the child and is responsible for the information entered.

Security and breaches

Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted. Access to records is limited by role (patient, doctor, front desk) and enforced in the database itself, so one patient cannot read another's records. Visit records keep an audit log. Supabase, which stores the data, states that it encrypts stored data and uses encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

On your device

The app keeps on your phone: your appearance setting, your sign-in session and a few flags that keep screens in the right state. The web version also lets your browser store app files so it opens quickly. If you turn on care reminders, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your phone from your care plan. Reminders are restored after your phone restarts. The reminder text does not contain medicine names. When you share a prescription as a PDF, the app creates a temporary file and deletes it after sharing; the app you share it with then holds its own copy. Signing out clears your session and your reminders.
