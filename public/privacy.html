Dr. Ashwani Family Clinic

App privacy policy

Information used by the clinic app

The app uses account and contact information, appointment requests, health readings, visit records, prescriptions and follow-up plans to operate your clinic account and support your care. It uses consultation order and payment status information to reconcile fees.

Service providers and sharing

Supabase provides sign-in and stores clinic app records. Google processes sign-in when you choose Continue with Google. Razorpay processes consultation payments. If you choose to share a prescription or open a consultation in WhatsApp, the destination app handles the information you send through it. The clinic app uses Cloudflare Pages for web hosting.

Information on your device

The app stores appearance preferences and may store session information, chat context, and clinical drafts on the device. If you enable care reminders, the app schedules them on your phone using your care plan.

Contact and account deletion

Email drashwanikansal@gmail.com about app privacy. You can also request deletion of your account and associated app data. The clinic will explain which clinical or payment records it must retain and for how long when handling your request.
