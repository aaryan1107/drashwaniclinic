# Theme

Tokens are defined in `src/styles.css`.

Core palette: navy `#09244a`, royal blue `#0f4c91`, electric blue `#1a6fd4`, emerald `#10b981`, amber `#f59e0b`, rose `#ef4444`, ink `#102033`, app background `#f4f8fd`, dark background `#06101c`.

Style direction: mobile-first clinic app, rounded phone shell, glass cards, strong contrast dark mode, safety/emergency states, compact medical workflows.
