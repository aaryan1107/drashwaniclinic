# Dr Ashwani Family Clinic: Brand Kit

The board version of this kit is [brand-board.html](brand-board.html), also on the Superdesign canvas (project "Dr. Ashwani Family Clinic — Launch Visuals").
Tokens live in `src/clinic-theme.css`; the brand layer (settled cards, rooms, eyebrows, buttons) is `src/clinic-brand.css`. Icons and splash screens are generated by
`scripts/build-brand-assets.py`. If you change the mark, change it there and in `index.html`, then re-run the script.

## 1. Strategy

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Family physician practice with a focus on long-term metabolic care (diabetes, thyroid, BP, weight) |
| **Audience** | Patients and families in Delhi NCR, many older or on low-end Android phones; one doctor running the clinic |
| **Promise** | The doctor knows your history and follows up. Care continues between visits. |
| **Personality** | Calm, exact, warm, unhurried. Like a good family doctor, not a hospital chain or a startup |
| **Avoid** | Hospital blue and red crosses, stock smiles, AI gradients, wellness pastels with no structure, alarm colours outside real alerts |
| **Line** | **Care that follows up.** |

## 2. The mark

A rounded two-tone cross on a teal tile.

- **The cross** says medicine without the emergency red cross, which is protected and reads as "ambulance".
- **Two tones** are the two people in every visit: the doctor (mint, vertical) and the patient (mist, horizontal). The doctor's bar sits on top, holding the patient's.
- **Fully rounded ends** keep it gentle and friendly at small sizes.

**Construction** (tile edge = 1):

- Horizontal bar: 0.56 × 0.19, radius 0.095, colour `#E0F0EC`
- Vertical bar: 0.19 × 0.56, radius 0.095, colour `#9FDCC0`, drawn on top
- App tile corner: 0.22 of the edge. iOS gets a square tile because the OS masks it.

**Rules**

- One mark in every theme. Only the colours change (see the table below). Never bring back the old heart, ring or leaf variants.
- Keep clear space of at least 0.25 of the tile edge around it.
- Minimum size: 20px on screen, 8mm in print.
- Do not outline it, add shadows or gradients, rotate it, or swap the bar order.
- The mark goes left of the wordmark, never above it inside the app header.

## 4. Type

- **Geist Variable**, served from the app bundle. One family for everything.
- Screen title 24px / 680 / -0.02em. Section 17px / 650. Body 16px. Caption 13px. No body text under 15px on phones.
- **Label style** (from prescription labels): 12-13px, 620 weight, uppercase, 0.09em tracking, muted. Use it for section eyebrows and the wordmark's "Family Clinic".
- Numbers (vitals, fees, dates) use tabular figures.

## 5. Shape and surfaces

- **Settled cards**: 14px radius, flat, no border, no shadow. Depth comes from fill contrast only: paper canvas, then card, then a tinted room. Shadows are only for things that float (sheets, menus).
- Hairlines (#E1E8E1) separate rows and headers, never outline cards.
- Controls: 12px radius, 52px tall for primary actions, 48px minimum touch target.
- **Rooms**: tinted card fills that group a kind of content: mint for care and next steps, sky for information, amber for things waiting on the patient, rose for alerts. A room is never the only signal; it always has a label.
- No cards inside cards.

## 6. Buttons

Built with `src/ui/Button.jsx` (Base UI Button + class-variance-authority).

| Intent | Look | Use |
|---|---|---|
| primary | Teal fill, paired ink | The one main action on a screen |
| secondary | Raised fill, control outline | Alternatives |
| ghost | Text only, teal | Low-stakes actions in rows |
| danger | Rose outline, rose text | Delete, cancel booking |

Press: scale 0.97 over 120ms. Loading: label stays, spinner replaces the icon, button is disabled.
Disabled: 50% opacity, no press. Focus: 2px action outline with 2px offset.

## 7. Voice

- Short, plain, second person. "Your next visit is on 4 Dec." not "Appointment scheduled successfully!"
- Name the doctor as "Dr Ashwani" in patient copy.
- Never blame: "We could not save this. Check your connection and try again."
- No em dashes in UI copy. No exclamation marks except greetings.
- Medical safety lines stay visible: decision support only; the doctor decides.

## 8. Imagery

Real clinic and doctor photos only, warm and daylight-lit. No stock patients. When there is no photo, use the mark on a mint room, not an illustration.
